Plant Care Tips

Simple tips to keep your plants healthy, fresh and beautiful.

Find Care Tips for Your Plant 🌿

Enter your plant name to discover how to care for it.

Watering Tips

Check the soil before watering. Water your plant when the top layer of soil feels dry.

Sunlight

Place plants according to their sunlight needs.Avoid exposing shade-loving plants to harsh sunlight.

Healthy Soil

Use well-draining soil so that excess water can escape and roots can breathe properly.

Leaf Cleaning

Gently wipe dusty leaves with a soft, damp cloth to help them stay clean.

Pruning

Remove dead or damaged leaves using clean scissors to support healthy growth.

Check for Pests

Regularly inspect leaves and stems for insects, spots or unusual changes.
